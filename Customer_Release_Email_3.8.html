Subject: Goals are here: Turn every conversation into progress

Product Release · November 2026

Turn every conversation
into progress.

We’ve launched Goals, a new feature that lets managers and their people set goals in each conversation and track progress against them, cycle after cycle. This release also brings Signals, clearer reports and stronger sign-in security.

Hi [First name],

Conversations are where managers and their people agree what to work on. With Goals, that agreement carries forward: Each goal keeps its progress, actions and check-ins, and its history is there at the next conversation. Here is what each change does for you, and where to find it.
🎯

Goals: Set them together, track them every cycle

What a manager and their person agreed to work on usually stayed in that one conversation. Checking whether it was on track meant asking again next time.

Now you can switch Goals on for any Enablement schedule and set up goal templates under Manage goals, so every conversation from that schedule starts with the right goals. Managers can add goals in the conversation, and employees can add their own. Goals starts switched off on every schedule, so nothing changes until you turn it on.

✓Target goals: Linked to one of the person’s measures, with progress read from the latest value, for example 108 of 120. When you set one up, you see how many people have a target for that measure.
✓Conversation goals: A written aim, with progress from the actions under it or from a progress bar the manager or employee moves.
✓Check-ins: The manager and employee each rate the goal’s likelihood, from Very likely to Very unlikely, and add a status.
✓A fresh start each cycle: When the next conversation is sent, the last cycle is kept as history. Goals added by hand can carry on with “Continue this goal”.
✓Goals in your Inbox: A new Goals view in the Inbox lists every conversation with goals.
📍 Settings ⚙ → Automation → Enablement → Goals  ·  Inbox → Goals
🔔

Signals: Spot who needs attention, sooner

Pulse results showed how people were tracking, but spotting a drop in someone’s scores or participation meant reading the reports and remembering to follow up.

Now Pulse Signals watch participation, risk factors, factor scores and comment sentiment on a schedule you set, and raise a signal when a person meets the conditions you choose. Managers see signals for their own people on the Signals page, and admins see every signal in the organisation.

✓One signal per issue: While a signal is still open, the next check updates it instead of adding another.
✓Context with every signal: Each one links to the pulse it came from, explains why it matters and includes Elli’s insight.
✓Straight to action: Create one ticket for a person that covers all their signals, or only the ones you tick.

Signals is switched on for each organisation separately. To turn it on for yours, reply to this email or book a walkthrough below.

📍 Signals in the side menu  ·  Set up in Settings ⚙ → Automation → Pulse Signals
📊

Reports: Clearer columns, sharper team views

Reports now explain what each column means, and pulse reports can focus on one team at every level.

✓Column tooltips: Column headers on the Performance Scorecard and Risk Signals reports explain what each column measures.
✓Lead Indicators is now Team Health: Report columns use the new name, and your saved reports keep working.
✓Team filter at every level: Managers can narrow Direct team or All reports to one team, and the progress, participation and overall score follow the filter.
✓Elli’s write-up for that team: The Executive Summary, Insights, Who to Talk to and Comments are written for the selected team, using only people you can already see.
📍 Reports in the side menu
🤝

Enablement: More control over who and what

Two changes help your Enablement schedules fit your organisation more closely.

✓Leave people out: Choosing Organisation now ticks everyone, and you can untick anyone who shouldn’t be included. People who join later aren’t added automatically.
✓Choose the questions for each review: Each Performance or Development Review schedule can ask only the library questions you tick. Schedules you leave as they are keep asking every question, including new ones.
📍 Settings ⚙ → Automation → Enablement
🔒

Security and sign-in: Stronger by default

We’ve tightened how Everperform handles sign-in, sessions and exported data.

✓Single sign-on (SAML): Admins can connect Okta, JumpCloud or another SAML 2.0 identity provider. It stays off until you add one, and our Customer team can help you set it up.
✓Change your password in the app: Do it from Settings ⚙ → Account → Details. Changing it signs you out on every other device.
✓Safer sessions: Resetting a password ends every session, logging out fully ends that session, and a session left unused for 30 days asks you to sign in again.
✓Safer CSV exports: Cells that start with =, +, - or @ now open as text in spreadsheets. This includes negative numbers, for example '-12.50.
✓Browser protection: Everperform now uses standard browser security settings, and its pages can no longer be shown inside other websites.
📍 SSO: Settings ⚙ → Organisation → SSO  ·  Admins only
