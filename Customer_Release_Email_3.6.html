We don't ship for the sake of shipping. Everything below exists to solve a real problem for you and your people — so rather than a changelog, here's the why, and where to find each one.
📨

Pulse Thank-You email

People fill in a pulse and hear… nothing. That silence quietly chips away at trust — and next time, fewer respond.

Now, the instant someone submits, they get a warm thank-you and answer receipt. A small gesture that says we heard you — and keeps response rates healthy.

📍 Automatic — sends the moment a pulse is completed
📊

Pulse Digest — the results come to you

Pulse insights used to sit in the platform, waiting to be found. The managers and leaders who could act on them had to remember to go looking.

Now, when a pulse closes, a tailored digest lands in the inbox of each audience — employee, manager, senior leader and admin — with a plain-English read on the findings, the signals that matter, and a clear next step. The story comes to the person who needs it.

📍 Automatic — emailed when a pulse closes
🗂️

Org Chart — your organisation, the way it really runs

Reporting lines quietly drive everything — who gets which pulse, whose results roll up where. When the structure is hard to see, it's hard to trust it's right.

Now there's a live view of your whole organisation — as a People tree, as Teams, or as a Network map — so you can see and sanity-check it in seconds.

📍 Settings ⚙ → Organisation → Chart  (admin)
[ Screenshot: Org Chart — People / Teams / Network ]
📈

Performance Measures — the right numbers, fast (productivity customers)

Finding one person's or team's measures used to mean scrolling past everyone else's.

Now you can filter Measures by reporting line — managers see their team, admins see the whole org, and you can drill to any individual. Even better: click a name in the Performance Scorecard or Risk Signals to jump straight to that person's Measures, with their name right at the top so you always know whose numbers you're reading.

📍 Reports → Measures  ·  or click a name in Scorecard / Risk Signals
[ Screenshot: Measures filtered to a person, name shown at top ]
🤖

Smart AI Insights & Actions — signals become accountable actions

You're sitting on rich pulse and safety data. Turning it into "so what do we do, and who owns it?" has been manual — and the easiest thing in the world to let slip.

Now ELI reads the signals for you, surfaces the insights that matter most, and can automatically raise a ready-to-action ticket for each — so every risk or opportunity becomes an owned, trackable action, not just another chart.

📍 Automation → Insight Alerts  ·  actions land in your Inbox
