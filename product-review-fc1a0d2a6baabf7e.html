What is working

AI quality — verified, not asserted

Customer feedback on the AI insights became a verified prompt and model change within a week, tested against source data before and after: invented content eliminated, and an evidence rule that blocks any statement the data cannot support. Rolling to every AI surface this cycle.

Fast feedback loops

Customer-support triage runs several times a week and feeds product directly. Feedback on the AI insights is meaningful and regular, and it is being turned into improvements progressively.

Resolution keeping pace

Intake roughly tripled in July and August against the preceding quarter. Resolution rose to match it by August.

Root cause over symptom

Where a class of issue recurs, the response is a structured logic review with tests pinned around intended behaviour so it cannot drift silently — not another point fix.

Area under active remediation

Automation scheduling reliability

Scheduled pulses and reminders depend on a scheduling layer that has, in a small number of cases, sent when switched off or fallen silent when switched on. Instances were fixed as they arose; the current work addresses the underlying logic as a whole and pins intended behaviour with tests. This is where product risk is concentrated today.

Also in focus. Two precision refinements to AI insight language are specified and scheduled.
