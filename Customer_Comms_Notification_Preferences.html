One page for every Everperform email

Five notifications, each with its own On/Off switch: the Pulse digest, the Pulse acknowledgement (the thank-you email), the Monthly summary, Inbox and Escalations.

✓Choose exactly who receives the digest, the Monthly summary, Inbox and Escalation emails. Search by name, or filter by Admins, Managers or Employees.
✓Set the Pulse digest per pulse, so each pulse reaches the group it matters to.
✓Limit Inbox and Escalation emails to the ticket types you choose.
✓Nothing changes until you press Save, and every change is recorded in the audit trail.
📍 Settings ⚙ → Organisation → Notifications  ·  admins only

The Pulse digest: your results, without logging in

When a pulse closes, the digest lands in your inbox with everything you need: participation, the overall score and how it moved, what ELI found across your teams, strengths and risk areas, priority recommendations, and who to talk to.

No reports to open and no filters to set. One email, and you know where to focus.

Example admin digest (Org Overview), from a demo organisation

🤝

Your managers get their own digest

Include your managers and each one receives a digest built from their own direct reports: how their team is going, what changed since the last pulse, and who needs a conversation. Leaders who oversee other managers also get a group view across all their teams.

This is direct feedback from their team, delivered to them. Managers can recognise what’s working and step in early where someone is struggling, without waiting for a report to be passed down.

Why it matters: when people see their feedback acted on, they keep giving it. Managers who see their team’s results are the fastest way to close that loop and lift engagement.
💌

Your employees see their own responses

Each employee who completes a pulse gets a thank-you email with a record of their own answers and their participation streak. It tells people their voice was heard, which keeps them responding next time.

Example thank-you email, from a demo organisation

Set it up in three steps

1
Log in and go to Settings → Organisation → Notifications.
2
Open the Pulse digest and pick the pulse schedules it covers.
3
Choose who receives it, including your managers, then press Save. It applies from the next pulse that closes.

Already happy with your emails? You don’t need to do anything. Your current settings stay exactly as they are until you save a change.
