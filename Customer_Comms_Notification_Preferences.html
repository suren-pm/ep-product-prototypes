Everperform sends a set of automated emails: Pulse results, thank-yous, a monthly scorecard report and ticket updates. With Notification Preferences, you decide which of them go out and who receives each one, so information reaches your people at a pace that works for your organisation.

For your managers, it means what they need to know about their people arrives in their inbox, without logging in to look for it. And as you set up new pulses and ticket workflows, you decide who hears about them from the same page.
🔔

New: Notification Preferences

One settings page for every automated email Everperform sends. Each email has its own toggle to switch it on or off, and you choose who receives it:

✓Choose who receives the Pulse digest for each pulse.
✓Pick recipients by name, or filter by Admins, Managers or Employees.
✓Limit Inbox and Escalation emails to the ticket types you choose.
✓Nothing changes until you press Save, and every change is recorded in the audit trail.
📍 Settings ⚙ → Organisation → Notifications  ·  admins only
📨

The emails you control

Pulse digest

Sent when a pulse closes, with participation, the overall score and how it moved, key findings, risk areas and who to talk to. Each person gets the results that are theirs to act on:

•Admins: The whole organisation.
•Senior leaders: A group view across the teams they oversee.
•Managers: Their own direct reports, and who needs a conversation.
•Employees who respond: Their own scores and insights on how they’re tracking.

Managers know where to step in, without logging in to look.

Pulse acknowledgement

Sent to each employee as soon as they complete a pulse, with the answers they just submitted to reflect on and their participation streak. People see their voice was heard, which keeps them taking part.

Monthly summary

The previous month’s scorecard report, sent at the beginning of each month.

Inbox

Emails the assignee and everyone else on a ticket when something changes: They’re added to it, someone comments, or its status, stage, priority or due date changes. Changes are held for 5 minutes from the first one, then sent as one email per ticket listing everything that changed, so a burst of edits arrives as a single email. Nobody is emailed about their own changes.

Escalations

Sent when someone escalates a ticket, either by ticking Escalate on the ticket or by escalating selected tickets in the Inbox. Tickets are only escalated by a person, never automatically. The email goes to the people on the ticket, including the person it’s about, limited to the recipients you choose. It follows the same 5-minute grouping as Inbox emails but has its own toggle, so escalations can stay on even when Inbox emails are off.

Send the Pulse digest to your managers in three steps

1
Log in and go to Settings → Organisation → Notifications.
2
Switch the Pulse digest toggle On. In the window that opens, pick a pulse.
3
Choose who receives its digest, for example all of your managers, and press Save. They’ll get their digest when the next pulse closes.

Happy with the emails your people get today? There’s nothing you need to do. Your current settings stay as they are until you save a change.
