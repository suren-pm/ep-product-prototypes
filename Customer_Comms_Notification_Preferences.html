A pulse survey is only as valuable as what happens after it closes. For feedback to lead to action, the results need to reach the people responsible for their teams while the feedback is still current, without anyone having to go looking for it.

This update is built around that moment. The Pulse digest gives your admins, managers and senior leaders a concise summary of each pulse, scoped to the people they are responsible for. Employees receive an acknowledgement and a record of their own responses. Inbox and Escalation emails keep the right people on top of tickets. And with the new Notification Preferences, you decide who receives each of these emails, along with everything else Everperform sends.
📨

Pulse digest: the results, without logging in

As soon as a pulse closes, the digest arrives by email with what you need to know: participation, the overall score and how it moved since the last pulse, the key findings, strengths and risk areas, priority recommendations, and who to talk to.

Each person sees the results that are theirs to act on:

✓Admins get the whole organisation, across every team.
✓Managers get their own direct reports: how their team is going and who needs a conversation.
✓Senior leaders get a group view across the teams they oversee.
✓Employees who respond get their own Pulse digest, with their personal scores and insights on how they’re tracking.
Why it matters for your managers: they hear directly from their team after every pulse, so they can recognise what’s working and step in early where someone is struggling. When people see their feedback acted on, they keep giving it.
💌

Pulse acknowledgement: a thank-you for every response

Each employee who completes a pulse gets a thank-you email with a record of their own answers and their participation streak. It shows people their voice was heard, which keeps them taking part in the next one.

📥

Inbox and Escalations: stay on top of tickets

Inbox emails let people know when there’s activity on their tickets: a new ticket, a reply or an update. Activity is grouped and sent after a short delay, so a busy day doesn’t mean a flood of emails.

Escalations go to a named group whenever a ticket escalates, separately from Inbox emails, so the people who need to step in hear about it straight away.

Both can be limited to the ticket types you choose.

🔔

New: Notification Preferences

Admins can now manage every automated Everperform email from a single settings page, including the Pulse digest, the Pulse acknowledgement, Inbox and Escalations. Each one has its own toggle to switch it on or off.

✓Choose who receives the Pulse digest for each pulse, so every pulse reaches the people it’s relevant to.
✓Pick recipients by name, or filter by Admins, Managers or Employees.
✓Limit Inbox and Escalation emails to the ticket types you choose.
✓Nothing changes until you press Save, and every change is recorded in the audit trail.
📍 Settings ⚙ → Organisation → Notifications  ·  admins only

To set up the Pulse digest, switch its toggle On. In the window that opens, pick a pulse, choose who receives its digest (for example, all of your managers), then press Save.

Send the Pulse digest to your managers in three steps

1
Log in and go to Settings → Organisation → Notifications.
2
Open the Pulse digest and pick the pulses it covers.
3
Add your managers as recipients and press Save. They’ll get their digest when the next pulse closes.

Happy with the emails your people get today? There’s nothing you need to do. Your current settings stay as they are until you save a change.
