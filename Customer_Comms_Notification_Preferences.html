Everperform sends a range of email notifications: Pulse results, acknowledgements, monthly scorecard reports, ticket updates and escalations. With Notification Preferences, you decide which of them go out and who receives each one, so information reaches your people at a pace that works for your organisation.

For your managers, it means what they need to know about their people arrives in their inbox, without logging in to look for it. And as you set up new pulses and ticket workflows, you decide who hears about them from the same page.
🔔

New: Notification Preferences

One settings page for every Everperform notification. Each notification has its own toggle to switch it on or off. You can also:

✓Choose who receives the Pulse digest for each pulse.
✓Pick recipients by name, or filter by Admins, Managers or Employees.
✓Limit Inbox and Escalation notifications to the ticket types you choose.
✓See every change in the audit trail. Nothing changes until you press Save.
📍 Settings ⚙ → Organisation → Notifications  ·  Admins only
📨

The notifications you control

Pulse digest

Sent when a pulse closes, showing participation, the overall score and how it has changed, key findings, risk areas and who to talk to. Each person gets the results that are theirs to act on:

•Admins: The whole organisation.
•Senior leaders: A group view across the teams they oversee.
•Managers: Their direct reports, including who needs a conversation.
•Employees who respond: Their own scores and insights on how they’re tracking.

Managers see where to step in without having to log in.

Pulse acknowledgement

Sent to each employee as soon as they complete a pulse. It includes their answers to reflect on and their participation streak, so people know their voice was heard and keep taking part.

Monthly summary

The previous month’s scorecard report, sent at the beginning of each month.

Inbox

Keeps everyone on a ticket up to date. An email is sent when someone is added to a ticket, a comment is posted, or the ticket’s status, stage, priority or due date changes. Changes made close together are grouped into one email per ticket, sent shortly after the update is made. Nobody is emailed about their own changes.

Escalations

Sent shortly after a ticket is escalated, either from the ticket itself or from the Inbox. It goes to the people on the ticket, including the person it’s about, and you can limit it to the recipients you choose. Escalations have their own toggle, separate from Inbox notifications, so they can stay on even when Inbox notifications are off.
