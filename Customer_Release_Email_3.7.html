Performance Reviews, rebuilt around real conversations

Reviews used to run on a fixed date with a fixed set of questions. Too often they felt like a form to get through, rather than a conversation between a manager and their person.

Now you set a review up once and it runs on your managers’ rhythm: weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly, on the day that suits them, such as the first Monday of the month. Send it to the whole organisation, chosen teams or chosen people. Write your own questions, rate on a simple 1 to 5 scale, and choose how much each side sees: transparent, partially confidential or fully confidential. Development Reviews work the same way.

✓Answers save as you go, and people can speak their answers instead of typing.
✓Managers see both sides of the conversation together before they respond.
✓ELI drafts the manager’s response from the person’s pulse results, scorecard and measures for the review period, written in a wellbeing-led tone. Managers edit it, keep it or try again.
✓A status view shows who has responded and who hasn’t.
📍 Settings ⚙ → Automation → Enablement
💬

Manager Conversations, at the moment they matter

The signs that someone needs a check-in, or deserves recognition, are usually already in the numbers. Spotting them relies on someone remembering to look.

Now a Manager Conversation can start automatically when a person’s measures meet the criteria you set, or on a schedule. Start from the built-in Recognition, Wellbeing or Accountability templates, or build your own, and ELI can suggest the questions to ask. When a full conversation isn’t needed, send the manager a quick email alert instead.

📍 Settings ⚙ → Automation → Enablement → New Enablement → Manager Conversation
🏠

Dashboard: see what needs you, the moment you sign in

Knowing where to focus meant opening report after report. It took several clicks just to find what needed your attention, before you could do anything about it.

Now admins and managers land on one page that shows what needs attention: overdue actions, risk registers waiting on someone, pulse participation, the latest recommendations from your pulses, and which areas are rising or falling. It only shows your own people, it follows the period you pick, and every line opens the exact item so you can act straight away.

📍 First in the side menu  ·  where you land when you sign in

Your first review cycle takes a few minutes to set up. Log in and head to Settings → Automation → Enablement to create it.
